This page documents how the RD3 Tech email templates use the current FIELD_SCHEMA. It is intended to be the working reference when changing Google Form fields, updating template tags, or modifying the AdminEmail and ClientEmail templates.


FIELD_SCHEMA is the source of truth. The relationship tables below are generated from the current schema rather than manually maintained lists.

How The Email Templates Work

The two templates have different purposes.

AdminEmail

Internal notification sent to RD3 Tech. It contains the submission details, client information, request information, and internal security/scope information.

INTERNAL

ClientEmail

Customer-facing confirmation. It confirms the enquiry, displays relevant submitted information, and explains what happens next.

CLIENT

Email Data Flow

Understanding this flow helps identify where a field should be changed.

1. Google Form

The visitor submits the form and Google supplies the submitted values and Entry IDs.

2. FIELD_SCHEMA

FIELD_SCHEMA identifies the field, its key, Entry ID, data type, label and aliases.

3. Request Data

The submission is normalised into the request/client objects used by the processing pipeline.

4. Email Template

AdminEmail and ClientEmail use template expressions to insert the appropriate values.

AdminEmail — SPAM, REVIEW and URGENT

These are separate conditions. A submission may trigger one, two or all three.

🟣 SPAM

Indicates that the automated security/spam evaluation has identified the submission as spam or suspicious.

safeSec.isSpam

AdminEmail displays SPAM DETECTED and lists the terms or triggers that caused the flag when those terms are available.

🟠 REVIEW

Indicates that the submission has triggered a configured scope/review condition.

safeSec.requiresReview

AdminEmail displays REQUIRES REVIEW and lists the matched review terms where available.

🔴 URGENT

Indicates that the submitted urgency has been marked High or Urgent.

safeReq.urgency === 'High' safeReq.urgency === 'Urgent'

AdminEmail displays URGENT REQUEST and identifies the submitted urgency level.

Changing a field name

Do not simply change the template tag. Update FIELD_SCHEMA first, then use the comparison table to identify all affected templates.

FIELD_SCHEMA

Changing security behaviour

SPAM and REVIEW conditions belong to the security/scope evaluation pipeline. The email template displays those results; it should not be the place where the security decision is made.
